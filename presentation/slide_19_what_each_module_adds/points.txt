WHAT EACH MODULE ADDS, ON ONE REAL DEPARTURE

(Plot: envelope_stages.png - IMU scale fault; columns 11.5 s, 4.8 s,
 2.0 s and 0.3 s before the vehicle left the road)

Row 1 - Layer 1 only: forecast + 90% conformal band. Follows the
  vehicle's corrupted estimate; the band is calibrated but in the wrong
  place. No notion of the road.
Row 2 - + 2a: counterfactual futures checked against the map. Still
  anchored to the corrupted estimate: P = 0.14 two seconds before the
  departure.
Row 3 - + 2b: the same futures moved by the estimated pose error. They
  land on the TRUE path: P = 1.00 two seconds before the departure.
Row 4 - + correction (Layer 3 preview): the vehicle's past path
  corrected by the same estimate (purple) lies on the true path
  (orange), not on the estimate (black).

Heading error at -2.0 s: true -13.9°, estimated -12.8°.
