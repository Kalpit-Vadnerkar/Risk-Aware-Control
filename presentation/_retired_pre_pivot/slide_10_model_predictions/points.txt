(Plot: prediction_example.png — position tied to the map, past/actual/
predicted trajectories, sparse outlined uncertainty circles; velocity/
steering/tl_state/tl_discrepancy panels with predicted mean +/- variance
band against actual)

Input/output sequence math:
  → Input: 30 past timesteps (3s at 10Hz) x 14 features + the HD map graph
  → Output: predicted distribution at each of 30 future timesteps
  → Position specifically: NOT absolute coordinates, NOT per-window
    bounding-box-relative (that was a real bug, see slide 6) — a
    fixed-scale displacement relative to "now" (the last input timestep),
    clamped to +/-100m, shared across every training example

What the plot shows working:
  → Predicted trajectory tracks the actual future closely under nominal
    driving
  → traffic_light_color and traffic_light_confidence (split from the old
    collapsed traffic_light_state) and traffic_light_discrepancy — all
    heads added/redesigned this project phase — now produce actual
    predicted distributions, where before this signal was only ever an
    input or a single conflated scalar

UPDATED 2026-08-06 — calibration is measurably NOT where we assumed
(new plots: coverage_curves.png, horizon_widening.png; full numbers in
docs/research_notes/trust_and_signal_behavior_2026-08-06.md):
  → std(z) near 1.0 (slide 6) was hiding a real problem — all 6 Gaussian
    heads are leptokurtic, not Gaussian (Anderson-Darling test decisively
    rejects normality for every one), so predicted intervals systematically
    overshoot their nominal confidence level
  → Predicted uncertainty only widens sensibly with prediction horizon for
    POSITION — velocity/steering/acceleration/both TL heads stay flat while
    actual error grows 2-4x by 3 seconds out
  → This is now the acknowledged blocking priority, ahead of resuming any
    SPRT/detection work or Autoware integration research — see slide 11
