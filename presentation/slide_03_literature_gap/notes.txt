Key point for Dr. Pisu: detection and trajectory-prediction accuracy are both mature, competitive literatures — publishing another detector or a marginally-more-accurate trajectory model would be incremental. The genuinely underserved combination is a MECHANISTIC signal (grounded in the HD map's own categorical assertions, not a generic anomaly score) paired with a DISTRIBUTION-FREE calibration guarantee and an actual LEAD-TIME measurement against a real safety outcome (not just a held-out test accuracy). Conformal prediction's theory is mature on its own, but it's rarely wired into an AV fault signal with a stated lead-time claim the way this project does in Stage 4.

Also worth citing directly: Seitzer et al.'s beta-NLL fix (ICLR 2022) was load-bearing this session — it directly fixed a real miscalibration bug found in this project's own Gaussian heteroscedastic heads (see docs/research_notes/trajectory_prediction_literature_2026.md), which is a nice concrete example of the trajectory-prediction literature actually informing this project's engineering, not just being cited for context.
