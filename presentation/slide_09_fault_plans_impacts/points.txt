FAULT PLANS AND IMPACTS

Fault plan (Plot: fault_plan_example_tl_fault_s3.png):
  → Shows a campaign's actual gating zone/radius overlaid on the route map
    before any data is collected — a visual sanity check that the fault is
    scoped to the right physical location (e.g., the one traffic light the
    vehicle's own route is governed by, not every light in the scene)

Fault impact (Plot: fault_impact_example_imu_fault_s3.png):
  → Overlays the nominal reference signal against the faulted trial's actual
    signal (e.g., EKF-vs-ground-truth divergence for IMU faults, TL
    color/confidence for TL faults), with the fault-active window shaded
  → Confirms the fault mechanism actually changed the signal it targets —
    not just that fault_log.jsonl recorded an event

Why this verification step matters:
  → A fault campaign can "run" (events logged, trial completes) without the
    injected fault ever actually reaching the signal it's supposed to affect
  → Every campaign in this project is checked this way before being trusted
    as input to any downstream model-reaction analysis
