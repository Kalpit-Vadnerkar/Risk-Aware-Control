FAULT PLANS AND IMPACTS: DOES THE INJECTION ACTUALLY WORK

Fault plan (Plot: fault_plan_example_tl_fault_s3.png):
  → Shows a campaign's actual gating zone/radius overlaid on the route
    map BEFORE any data is collected — a visual sanity check that the
    fault is scoped to the right physical location (the one traffic
    light the vehicle's own route is governed by, not every light in the
    scene)

Fault impact, message level (Plot: fault_impact_example_imu_fault_s3.png):
  → Overlays the nominal reference signal against the faulted trial's
    actual signal (EKF-vs-ground-truth divergence for IMU faults, TL
    color/confidence for TL faults), fault-active window shaded
  → Confirms the fault mechanism actually changed the raw signal it
    targets — not just that fault_log.jsonl recorded an event

Why this verification step matters, and still matters:
  → A fault campaign can "run" (events logged, trial completes) without
    the injected fault ever reaching the signal it's supposed to affect
    — this has actually happened before in this project's history (a
    topic-name bug once made an entire campaign silently a no-op) and is
    exactly the kind of failure this check catches
  → Every campaign is verified this way before being trusted as input to
    any downstream analysis — including everything on slide 13

This is necessary but not sufficient: confirming the fault changes the
RAW SIGNAL is a different question from whether the CALIBRATED MODEL's
own uncertainty notices — that's the new result on slide 13.
