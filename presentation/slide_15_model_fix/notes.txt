A "good enough for safety" benchmark gate was added for this: off-road
<= 2%, lateral error p50 <= 0.5 m, phantom and wrong-direction turns <=
5%. It doubles as the ablation table for the paper (one row per model,
experiments/analysis/model_safety_benchmark/benchmark_table.md).

Be honest that the off-road rate sits at 1.7-3.2% depending on the
training run: right at the gate, not comfortably under it. It does not
drive the Layer 2 results (the nominal envelope is calibrated on the
model's own errors), but it is the first thing a reviewer will ask.

If asked "why not keep the original model and recalibrate": a wider
interval around a forecast that is on the wrong road does not fix the
forecast - same argument as the slide 11 turn fix last time.
