The held-out-calibration-set caveat is the single most important honest
caveat in the whole deck — say it plainly here and don't let it come as a
surprise on slide 14. It was found by checking, directly, which specific
trial files landed in the training split vs. the calibration split (not
assumed): goal_026's two nominal trials are properly held out, goal_007
and goal_012's are not (used in training). This directly limited how much
of the 2026-08-25 TL severity pilot could be trusted (slide 13) and is
exactly what the upcoming lab session is designed to fix (slide 15) —
new nominal trials at goal_007/012, with an explicit verification step
this time to make sure they actually land in the held-out set.

The IMU-vs-TL failure-signature distinction (state estimation vs.
perception value) is worth setting up clearly here since it's the
throughline for slide 13's finding and slide 15's Layer 2 scoping
decision — don't let it feel like a surprise introduced late.

If asked why only 3 goals (007/012/026) get fault campaigns instead of
all 26: cost — each fault campaign at each goal is a full AWSIM trial,
and 8 campaigns x 26 goals was never going to be tractable; 3 goals were
chosen to give real route diversity (different turn/intersection
geometry) without an unbounded collection cost.
