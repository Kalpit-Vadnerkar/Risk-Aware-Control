imu_fault_s1 is deliberately a negative control, not an oversight — 0.03 rad/s gyro bias is designed to be absorbed by EKF/localization's noise-rejection band even during a turn, so a LOW detection rate on this campaign specifically is a pass, not a miss (see slide 11's results table, color-coded accordingly).

tl_fault_s3 (tl_unknown) and tl_fault_s4 (tl_blackout) are the two campaigns where perception genuinely stops reporting a categorical fact the map asserts should be there — the actual negative-evidence case the mechanism (slide 5) is built around. tl_fault_s2 (oscillate) and tl_fault_ramp (confidence decay) are real faults but NOT negative-evidence cases — perception still reports a present value, just a wrong or degrading one. This distinction turned out to matter a lot in this session's discriminability results (slide 11) — worth setting up here so it lands cleanly later in the deck.

The zone-file expansion (3 goals -> 26) is a good example of genuine methodology tightening that required zero new data collection, which matters practically since Kalpit can't run new AWSIM/Autoware sessions for a while — this was the first thing tackled this session for exactly that reason.
