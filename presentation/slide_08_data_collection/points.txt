DATA COLLECTED, AND THE FAULT TAXONOMY

Nominal baseline (nom_v11): 26 goals across the map, ~2 trials each.
(Plot: route_map_example_goal012.png — one goal's route over the HD map,
traffic-light zones marked)

IMPORTANT CAVEAT, stated plainly: only 7 of these trials currently sit in
the HELD-OUT calibration set the model has never seen — the rest were
used in training. This is the single biggest data-scale limitation on
every coverage-guarantee claim in this update (flagged independently by
three separate checks this cycle) — more nominal trials is not optional
polish, it's load-bearing for how much these results can be trusted.

Fault campaigns (8 total + 3 new this cycle, all on goals 007/012/026):

  IMU faults (state-estimation corruption)   | TL faults (perception spoofing)
  --------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------
  imu_fault_s1    — mild bias (control)       | tl_fault_s2 — oscillating color (5s period)
  imu_fault_s3    — stronger bias              | tl_fault_s3 — UNKNOWN classification
  imu_fault_scale — gyro scale error           | tl_fault_s4 — full signal blackout
  imu_fault_stuck — gyro frozen                | tl_fault_ramp — confidence decays 1.0->0.0
                                                | tl_fault_fixed_030/050/070 — NEW: confidence
                                                |   FIXED at severity 0.3/0.5/0.7 for a whole
                                                |   zone dwell (why fixed, not ramped: slide 15)

All fault campaigns gate on a route-derived zone (turn zone, bias lead-in
zone, or traffic-light zone) computed from the map + planned route — not
a live threshold or a wall-clock guess, and the exact same geometry used
for the calibration audit (slide 10).

Two fundamentally different failure signatures, previewed here, shown in
full on slide 13: IMU faults corrupt STATE ESTIMATION (a slow,
compounding, non-recovering effect); TL faults corrupt a PERCEPTION VALUE
(a fast, contained effect). This difference turns out to matter a lot for
how consequence estimation (Layer 2) needs to be scoped.
