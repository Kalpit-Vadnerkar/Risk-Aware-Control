HOW MUCH AND WHICH WAY: THE ESTIMATE TRACKS GROUND TRUTH

(Plot: heading_tracking.png - all 8 IMU fault trials; black = true
 heading error from AWSIM ground truth, purple = 2b's estimate)

  → On the 4 real departures, estimate and truth overlap to within a few
    degrees, up to +/-200° (stuck gyro)
  → Harmless faults (s1): truth ~2°, estimate 0-1.4°. LiDAR matching is
    healthy there, so the estimator trusts it and pulls toward zero -
    the right side to err on when the error is too small to matter
    (the previous mechanism read 2-5°, too high)
  → Nothing here was fitted to fault data: the consistency heads are
    trained and calibrated on nominal driving only
