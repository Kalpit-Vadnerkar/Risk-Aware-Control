This slide fully replaces the old belief-divergence/epistemic-stance
mechanism — not a patch, a real architectural change. The old mechanism
was specific to the traffic-light channel (an HD-map categorical fact vs.
perception's report) and needed a name for what kind of fault to look
for. This framework needs neither: Layer 1's calibration is built from
the model's own point predictions across every feature, and needs no
fault data at all to construct (only real faults to VALIDATE it against
afterward — slide 13). That's a strictly more general mechanism, not a
narrower replacement for a special case.
