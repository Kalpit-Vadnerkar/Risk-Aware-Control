THE FRAMEWORK: FROM CALIBRATED SURPRISE TO PHYSICAL CONSEQUENCE
(revised 2026-10 after the 2026-09-03 feedback; full diagram on slide 17)

  Observed past + HD map + redundant ego sources
                       │
                       ▼
  LAYER 1 - DETECT: one ST-GAT, trained and calibrated on nominal driving
    behaviour heads    -> WHEN the state / forecast stops being nominal
    consistency heads  -> HOW MUCH and WHICH WAY the state estimate is off
    every residual conformally calibrated (leave-one-trial-out)
                       │
                       ▼
  LAYER 2 - CONSEQUENCE: shape Layer 1's envelope, never build a second one
    2a  a priori: scene-conditioned counterfactual futures
        (forecast + recorded errors of the most similar scenes)
    2b  a posteriori: move the envelope by the estimated pose error,
        so it grows while a fault goes unmitigated
    2c  safe sets on the real map: road edge, other road users
        -> P(violation within 3 s) = share of futures that violate
                       │
                       ▼
  LAYER 3 - RESPOND: correct the state, slow down, or stop

Why this is the gap (slide 3): UQ work stops at an alarm magnitude;
risk-aware planning assumes its own prediction is correct. Here the SAME
calibrated signal is carried through to a map-grounded probability, and
the evidence that sizes it is what a response can act on.

Two intuitions:
  → A calibrated interval alone doesn't know when it MATTERS; Layer 2
    puts it on the map
  → An anomaly score says something is wrong; only a second measurement
    of the same quantity says how much and which way (slide 16)
