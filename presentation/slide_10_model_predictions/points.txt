MODEL PREDICTIONS

(Plot: prediction_example.png — position tied to the map, past/actual/
predicted trajectories, sparse outlined uncertainty circles; velocity/
steering/tl_state/tl_discrepancy panels with predicted mean +/- variance
band against actual)

Input/output sequence math:
  → Input: 30 past timesteps (3s at 10Hz) x 14 features + the HD map graph
  → Output: predicted distribution at each of 30 future timesteps
  → Position specifically: NOT absolute coordinates, NOT per-window
    bounding-box-relative (that was a real bug, see slide 6) — a
    fixed-scale displacement relative to "now" (the last input timestep),
    clamped to +/-100m, shared across every training example

What the plot shows working:
  → Predicted trajectory tracks the actual future closely under nominal
    driving, with uncertainty widening sensibly at longer horizons
  → traffic_light_state and traffic_light_discrepancy — the two heads
    added this session — now produce an actual predicted distribution,
    where before this signal was only ever an input, never predicted

Calibration is real but imperfect (verified via z-score diagnostics on held-
out data, not assumed): position mildly underconfident, velocity/steering/
tl_state mildly-to-moderately overconfident — see slide 6 for the numbers
and the fixes already applied this session.
