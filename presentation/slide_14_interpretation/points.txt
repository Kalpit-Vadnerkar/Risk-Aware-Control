INTERPRETATION: WHAT THIS ACTUALLY SUPPORTS, AND WHAT IT DOESN'T YET

What's solid:
  → The calibration methodology itself (conformal over a point predictor)
    is validated, reproducible, and the right replacement for the
    abandoned distributional-head approach — not in question
  → The audit-then-fix methodology (find a hidden per-scenario gap,
    close it at the training level, validate the closure both
    quantitatively and on concrete examples) worked exactly as intended
  → The calibrated signal demonstrably responds to real injected faults,
    with a real, likely-novel finding about which features carry the
    clearest evidence

What's real but needs to be stated as a limitation, not glossed over:
  → Only 7 nominal trials currently sit in the held-out calibration set
    — flagged independently by three separate checks this cycle as
    load-bearing-weak for the coverage-guarantee claims specifically,
    not optional polish. A concrete fix is already planned (slide 15).
  → The TL severity-sweep result so far is a promising PILOT, not the
    paper's headline result — a real confound was found and understood,
    and the design for the clean version already exists.
  → Mondrian conditional calibration is not a strictly better default
    than pooled calibration — say this precisely if asked, it's a real,
    quantified trade-off, not a caveat to bury.

Where this leaves the novelty claim, checked independently (not just
self-assessed): the specific combination here — geometry-grounded audit +
training-level fix + honest discrete-vs-continuous calibration comparison
+ real fault validation with cross-feature diagnosticity — was not found
anywhere in the literature by a fresh reviewer explicitly tasked to argue
against it. Verdict: dissertation-worthy as Paper 1, once the severity
sweep and more nominal data land.

What this means for direction: Layer 1 is close enough to finished that
the next real payoff is Layer 2 (consequence estimation) — not more
Layer 1 refinement for its own sake. The two remaining Layer 1 items
(severity sweep, more data) are scoped, planned, and don't block starting
Layer 2 design work in parallel.
