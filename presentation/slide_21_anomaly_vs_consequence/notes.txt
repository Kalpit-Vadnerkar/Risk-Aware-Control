Lead time is bounded by the 3 s forecast horizon (P is "violation
within 3 s"), which is why it clusters at 2-3.4 s. Layer 1 alone warns
earlier (4-10 s) because it flags the anomaly, not the danger.

If asked about s3/goal_012: the previous iteration showed 18% there, but
only because its drifting position estimate moved the envelope AWAY
from the tight spot - an artifact. With the formal-model filter the
envelope stays put, and the alarm is the nominal risk of standing at
that spot. Separating "risk of the scene" from "risk added by the
fault" is what the dP variant does (0.22 there).
