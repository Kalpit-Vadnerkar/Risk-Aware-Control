This is the key conceptual finding of the cycle - say it slowly.

"When" = behavioural residuals of any channel. "How much / which way" =
disagreement between two independent measurements of the same physical
quantity (pose-derived yaw rate vs the vehicle's own yaw rate; pose
speed vs wheel speed; LiDAR-to-map matching health).

A bug found on the way and worth one sentence if asked: AWSIM reports
the vehicle yaw rate with the opposite sign to ROS, which had made the
first consistency channel twice the yaw rate. Verified (correlation
-0.998) and fixed.
