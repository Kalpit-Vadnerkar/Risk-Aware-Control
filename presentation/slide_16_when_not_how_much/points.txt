SECOND PROBLEM: RESIDUALS TELL YOU WHEN, NOT HOW MUCH OR WHICH WAY

The obvious way to couple Layer 1 into Layer 2: grow the envelope in
proportion to the residuals. It does not work.

(Plot: coupling_ladder.png - real road departures warned, of 4)

  → Scale the residuals by the anomaly                 0/4
  → Residual-triggered symmetric "trust region"        0/4
  → Hand-built dead-reckoning pose check (baseline)    4/4
  → Learned consistency heads (this work)              4/4

Why: a behavioural residual compares the forecast with ONE source - the
corrupted estimate itself. It reliably says something is wrong 2-4 s
before the departures, but it underestimates the true pose error about
10x and its sign flips between trials. The estimated trajectory cannot
reveal its own error.

Size and direction need a second, independent measurement of the same
quantity. That is the design principle for the rest of the architecture.
