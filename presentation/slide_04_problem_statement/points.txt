PROBLEM STATEMENT & CONTRIBUTION

One-sentence thesis:
  A digital twin's own predictive uncertainty, once properly calibrated
  and validated against real faults, is the substrate for translating
  "how off-nominal is this vehicle behaving" into "what physical
  consequence does that imply" — without needing a catalog of known fault
  types to work.

Three-layer architecture:
  (1) DETECT — a continuous, calibrated anomaly signal. No fault data
      needed to build. THIS UPDATE'S FOCUS — nearly complete.
  (2) CONSEQUENCE — roll that signal forward through the digital twin
      against a physical safety margin (reachability-based). THE CORE
      REMAINING THESIS WORK — not yet built, scoped next (slide 15).
  (3) RESPOND — graceful behavioral modulation. Explicit future work,
      out of current scope.

What's already banked (from the T-ITS 2025 paper, not re-argued here):
  → Fault detection itself: 93.7% accuracy, Camera/IMU/LiDAR faults
  → Traffic Light Status Flag as the dominant feature (29.7% importance)

What this update adds (Layer 1, the actual new claim):
  (1) A calibrated point predictor with a real, distribution-free
      coverage guarantee (conformal prediction) — not a jointly-trained
      distributional head, which was tried and demonstrably failed to
      calibrate (slide 6)
  (2) Proof that a POOLED guarantee can hide a real per-scenario failure
      — found, then FIXED at the model-training level, not just
      recalibrated around (slides 10-11)
  (3) Two competing ways to make the calibrated interval scenario-aware,
      compared honestly, trade-offs stated precisely (slide 12)
  (4) Validation against REAL injected sensor/perception faults in the
      live simulator — the calibrated signal responds, and not always
      through the feature you'd expect (slide 13)

Scoping decision: split into two papers, not one.
  → Paper 1 (Layer 1, this update): calibration + audit + fix + fault
    validation — independently verdicted as dissertation-worthy on its
    own, once a severity sweep and more nominal data land
  → Paper 2 (Layer 2): consequence estimation, building on Paper 1's
    validated calibration as its input — a distinct mechanism, not a
    fragment of one story
