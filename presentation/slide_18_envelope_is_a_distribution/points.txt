WHAT THE ENVELOPE IS: A DISTRIBUTION, CARRIED BY SAMPLES

(Plot: envelope_explainer.png - one moment, 2 s before a real departure)

  a. 200 counterfactual futures: the ST-GAT forecast plus ONE real
     recorded error trajectory each, drawn from the 150 calibration
     windows most similar to this scene (the model's own embedding)
  b. Together they are an empirical distribution over future paths -
     drawn here as a density with 50% / 90% regions at 3 s
  c. 2b moves that distribution: each sample is rotated and shifted by a
     draw of the estimated pose error (mean = which way, spread = how much)
  d. P(violation) = the share of the distribution that leaves the road
     (here 200 of 200)

Why samples and not a Gaussian tube:
  → Errors are correlated over the horizon (a car 1 m left at 1 s is
    still left at 2 s); whole recorded trajectories keep that shape
  → The safe set is a map region, not an ellipse: counting samples
    outside it is the direct Monte Carlo estimate of the probability
  → No distribution family assumed - same reason conformal replaced the
    Student-t heads
