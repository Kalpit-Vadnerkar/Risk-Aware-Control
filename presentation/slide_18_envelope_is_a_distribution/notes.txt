If asked "is the bunch of trajectories the data?": no - each line is a
sample from a distribution. The randomness has two sources: which
recorded error trajectory (non-parametric, from real calibration data,
conditioned on the scene) and which pose error (Gaussian with the 2b
mean and calibrated spread). 200 samples give P to about +/-0.03 at
P = 0.5.

The orange dashed line is the TRUE path from AWSIM ground truth. In
panel c the moved envelope sits on the true path, while the forecast
(blue) follows the corrupted estimate.
