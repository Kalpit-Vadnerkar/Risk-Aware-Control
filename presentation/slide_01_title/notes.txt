Direction reframed since the last meeting (2026-07-24 pivot, see docs/theoretical_framework.md). Detection itself is already banked from the published T-ITS 2025 paper (93.7% fault detection accuracy across Camera/IMU/LiDAR). This chapter's contribution is verification, not detection: does the digital twin's divergence signal come with a calibrated confidence guarantee, how much lead time does it give before a fault becomes safety-relevant, and how does that lead time trade off against fault severity. The prior "avoidance vs. MRC decision" framing from the last presentation has been retired — it was a control-layer extension (RISE) that turned out not to be the core defensible claim; this deck presents the replacement direction end to end.
