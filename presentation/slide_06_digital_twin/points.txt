THE DIGITAL TWIN: ST-GAT + CONFORMAL CALIBRATION (revised this cycle)

Encoder: graph attention over the HD-map lanelet graph (route-aware node
selection, learned attention pooling — not a uniform mean) + LSTM temporal
encoder over a 3-second (30-step, 10Hz) window, + a permutation-invariant
object-set encoder (up to 8 tracked objects, masked attention pooling).

Predicted features (7, point predictions — mean only):
  → position, velocity (longitudinal/lateral split), steering,
    acceleration, traffic-light color, traffic-light confidence

Per-horizon-step decoding: each feature's head takes [shared hidden state
; that feature's OWN learned horizon-step embedding] — fixed a real bug
this cycle where every head shared ONE embedding table and effectively
fought over it (route-ahead position information owned 40-44% of that
table's gradient, actively hurting every other head).

THE APPROACH THAT WAS TRIED AND ABANDONED — worth stating plainly, not
glossing over:
  → Originally: jointly-trained Student-t distributional heads
    (mean+scale+dof), the "obvious" way to get uncertainty from a neural
    predictor
  → Measurably failed to calibrate: coverage-curve max gap 0.066-0.376,
    a statistical test (KS) decisively rejects calibration for every
    feature — the model's own reported confidence didn't mean what it
    claimed
  → Root cause: a known pathology in jointly training a network to BOTH
    predict AND self-report confidence in one objective (dof-collapse,
    scale/residual mismatch)

THE FIX — conformal calibration wraps the point predictor instead:
  → No joint optimization, no distribution-family assumption to fight
  → Take the plain mean-only predictor (point accuracy was solid the
    whole time — the problem was never accuracy) and derive a calibrated
    interval directly from held-out residuals
  → Result: coverage-curve max gap 0.004-0.011 — a decisive calibration
    improvement, even though point accuracy is actually MIXED (the
    abandoned approach is slightly better on 4/6 features) — say this
    plainly, calibration quality is the reason for the switch, not
    accuracy
