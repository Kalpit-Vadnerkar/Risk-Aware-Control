Layer 1 - DETECT (trained and calibrated on nominal driving only)
  → One ST-GAT with two kinds of heads:
      behaviour heads (position, velocity, steering, acceleration,
      traffic light) -> WHEN
      consistency heads (estimated pose vs the vehicle's own yaw rate,
      along-track and lateral speed) -> HOW MUCH, WHICH WAY
  → Every residual is a conformal p-value; combined into one bounded
    score; onset by a capped CUSUM with a recovery test

Layer 2 - CONSEQUENCE
  2a  scene-conditioned envelope: forecast + real recorded errors of the
      150 most similar scenes (the calibrated tube, shaped a priori)
  2b  evidence growth: the envelope is moved by the estimated pose error
      (shaped a posteriori), so P grows while a fault goes unmitigated
  2c  safe sets: road edge, other road users (stop line, speed: planned)

Layer 3 - RESPONSE: correct the state, slow down, or stop (slide 24)
