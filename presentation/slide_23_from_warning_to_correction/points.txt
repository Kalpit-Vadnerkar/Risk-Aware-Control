FROM WARNING TO CORRECTION

Knowing how much and which way means we can subtract the error, not
just warn about it.

(Plot: correction_offline.png - Autoware's own pose error vs the error
 left after subtracting the 2b estimate; open loop, against AWSIM truth)

At the moment of departure:
                 heading            lateral
  scale/007      22.7° -> 1.3°      3.5 m -> 1.2 m
  s3/007         23.2° -> 1.0°      2.7 m -> 1.0 m
  stuck/007      95.4° -> 1.7°      5.8 m -> 0.8 m
  stuck/012      26.3° -> 2.6°      0.8 m -> 0.4 m

Caveats, stated plainly:
  → Open loop: the vehicle did not use the correction. Whether it would
    have stayed on the road needs the closed-loop experiment (slide 24)
  → Correct only when Layer 1 says the state is off: on the harmless s1
    fault, correcting raises the lateral error from 0.1 to 0.4 m
