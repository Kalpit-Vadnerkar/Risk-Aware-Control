Risk-Aware Control Architecture

How the pipeline turns "my state or forecast is off-nominal" into a calibrated probability of a safety violation that grows while a fault goes unmitigated. Current state, not the plan of record for every box.

Updated 2026-10-07 Model in use: ego_v5 (training) Design document

Status key

  • builtImplemented and measured on the current data.
  • in progressBeing built or retrained now (new, iterating, in training).
  • under reviewWorks, but the design is being reconsidered: the alarm and onset logic, and how results are reported.
  • plannedSpecified in the design, not built yet.
  • openScope not decided.

Recent changes

  • 2026-10-07Vehicle yaw-rate sign fixed: AWSIM reports it opposite to ROS, which had made the first consistency channel twice the yaw rate.
  • 2026-10-07Onset moved from fixed-hold flags to a calibrated CUSUM; its saturation under large faults is now under review, with the alarm strategy.
  • 2026-10-07Redundant ego-state sources moved inside the predictor as inputs and consistency heads (ego_v4).
  • 2026-10-05Ego-frame map, route fix and route-ahead input (ego_v3); predictor lateral error at 3 s down from 0.98 m to 0.13 m.
